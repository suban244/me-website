## Money Personalities
People with money fall in one of 4 catagories based on how they use the money
1. Spender: 
    - Spending most of the money with little regard to future.
    - One interesting note here is that the **Non thinker**(A person who doesn't want to think about money) tends to be quite closely related to this
    - One useful advice for tends to be setting aside some amount before anything.
2. Saver:
    - Prioritizes the saving of money before all else.
    - Learning to spend money is often time also a important skill, which sorta gets missted here. as well as living life a little.
3. Investor: 
    - Wiling to take a little risk with their money with the hopes of making it grow.
4. Balancer:
    - A mix of the 3.

## Psychology of Money
There are also multiple biases that influence how we spend our money. Being aware of these can help us more money more smartly.

#### Herd Mentality
The tendency of making financial decisions based on others, without any personal research. 
There are two ways this can happen
1. Everyone is putting money here, it must be good, or else i will miss out.
2. Everyone is buying this, I think I need it too.
Following the herd tends to free us from **thinking** about the the money is being used, which can often lead to bad financial decisions.
